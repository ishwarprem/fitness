BACK MUSCLES

UPPER BACK

Pull-Ups (Wide Grip)

3-4 sets of 8-12 reps. Targets upper lats and teres major for width.

Face Pulls

3 sets of 15-20 reps. Excellent for rear delts and upper back thickness.

High Cable Rows

3 sets of 10-12 reps. Builds upper back density and trapezius.

MID BACK

Barbell Rows

4 sets of 8-10 reps. Core exercise for mid-back thickness.

T-Bar Rows

3-4 sets of 8-12 reps. Great for middle back development.

Seated Cable Rows

3 sets of 10-12 reps. Isolates middle back muscles effectively.

LOWER BACK

Deadlifts

3-4 sets of 5-8 reps. The king of lower back and posterior chain development.

Hyperextensions

3 sets of 12-15 reps. Strengthens erector spinae and lower back.

Good Mornings

3 sets of 10-12 reps. Builds lower back strength and hamstring flexibility.

BICEPS MUSCLES

LONG HEAD

Incline Dumbbell Curls

3-4 sets of 10-12 reps. Stretches and targets the long head for peak development.

Wide Grip Barbell Curls

3 sets of 8-12 reps. Emphasizes long head activation.

SHORT HEAD

Close Grip EZ Bar Curls

3-4 sets of 10-12 reps. Targets inner biceps for thickness.

Concentration Curls

3 sets of 10-15 reps. Maximum isolation of short head.

BRACHIALIS

Hammer Curls

3 sets of 10-12 reps. Primary exercise for brachialis thickness.

Reverse Curls

3 sets of 10-12 reps. Works brachialis and brachioradialis.

TRICEPS MUSCLES

LONG HEAD

Overhead Tricep Extensions

3-4 sets of 10-12 reps. Primary exercise for long head development.

Skull Crushers

3 sets of 8-12 reps. Excellent mass builder for long head.

LATERAL HEAD

Cable Pushdowns (Rope)

3 sets of 12-15 reps. Isolates lateral head for definition.

Dips

3-4 sets of 8-12 reps. Great for lateral head development.

MEDIAL HEAD

Close-Grip Bench Press

4 sets of 8-10 reps. Compound movement hitting all three heads.

Reverse Grip Pushdowns

3 sets of 12-15 reps. Emphasizes medial head activation.

SHOULDER MUSCLES

ANTERIOR DELTOID

Front Raises

3 sets of 10-12 reps. Isolates front deltoids.

Military Press

4 sets of 8-10 reps. Primary mass builder for front delts.

LATERAL DELTOID

Lateral Raises

3-4 sets of 12-15 reps. Essential for shoulder width.

Upright Rows

3 sets of 10-12 reps. Builds lateral deltoid mass.

POSTERIOR DELTOID

Rear Delt Flyes

3 sets of 12-15 reps. Critical for rear delt development.

Face Pulls

3 sets of 15-20 reps. Excellent for rear delts and shoulder health.

FOREARM MUSCLES

FLEXORS

Wrist Curls

3 sets of 15-20 reps. Builds forearm flexor mass.

Grip Crushers

Perform 2 to 3 sets of 8-15 reps on each hand, focusing on controlled and maximal squeezing.

EXTENSORS

Reverse Wrist Curls

3 sets of 15-20 reps. Targets forearm extensors.

Wrist Rollers

3 sets to failure. Complete forearm extensor workout.

GRIP STRENGTH

Farmer's Walks

3 sets of 30-60 seconds. Builds overall forearm and grip strength.

Dead Hangs

3 sets of max time. Develops grip endurance and strength.

LEG MUSCLES

QUADRICEPS

Barbell Squats

4 sets of 8-12 reps. The king of quad development.

Leg Press

3-4 sets of 10-15 reps. Great for quad mass with less spinal load.

Leg Extensions

3 sets of 12-15 reps. Isolates quadriceps for definition.

HAMSTRINGS

Romanian Deadlifts

3-4 sets of 8-10 reps. Primary hamstring mass builder.

Leg Curls

3 sets of 12-15 reps. Isolates hamstrings effectively.

Stiff-Leg Deadlifts

3 sets of 10-12 reps. Stretches and builds hamstrings.

GLUTES

Hip Thrusts

3-4 sets of 10-12 reps. Best exercise for glute development.

Bulgarian Split Squats

3 sets of 10-12 reps per leg. Builds glutes and overall leg strength.

CALVES

Standing Calf Raises

4 sets of 15-20 reps. Targets gastrocnemius for calf mass.

Seated Calf Raises

3 sets of 15-20 reps. Works soleus for complete calf development.
